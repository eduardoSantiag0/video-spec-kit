# Storyboard — Night ride in the rain (scene-001 · v002)

**Intent:** A quiet, lonely moment of determination — one tired rider pushing through a storm in a glowing city.
**Format:** 5 s · 16:9 · 24 fps · text-to-video
**Camera:** medium, eye-level, 35mm, tracking-side (moderate)

## First frame (0.0 s)

Medium side view of the rider mid-pedal, centered, raincoat glistening, neon
signs softly blurred behind her, rain streaks crossing the frame.

## Beats

| Time      | What happens on screen | Camera | Notes |
|-----------|------------------------|--------|-------|
| 0.0–2.5 s | She pedals steadily through the rain and wipes rain from her brow with her right hand, then returns it to the handlebar. | Tracks alongside from the left at her speed | One-handed action near the face: hand instability risk |
| 2.5–5.0 s | She keeps her eyes on the road ahead and leans slightly into the pedals as neon light slides across her raincoat. | Keeps tracking, framing unchanged | Face angle constant (v002 change) |

## Last frame (5.0 s)

The rider still centered and pedaling, eyes on the road, both hands on the
handlebars, magenta and cyan neon reflected in the wet street behind her.

## Continuity

- Previous scene: none
- Next scene: none
- Must remain consistent: Mika's locked traits (hair, raincoat, backpack)

## Design notes

- Tracking alongside shows the rider and the city at once, which serves "one person against a big glowing city".
- v002: the glance toward camera was removed (identity drift in v001). The brow wipe stays until it is tested on its own.
- The last frame keeps her centered with both hands on the bars, so a next scene can continue from it.
